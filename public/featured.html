Lerato M.

Lerato M.

Acrylic portrait artist specializing in emotive black-and-white portraiture. Based in Johannesburg with over 8 years of experience exploring themes of identity, heritage, and human connection.

Her signature cracked glass technique was developed during a residency in Cape Town, where she experimented with layering acrylics to create texture that mimics the fragility and resilience of human emotion.
