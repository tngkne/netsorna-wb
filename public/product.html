Lerato M.

Acrylic Portrait Artist

Based in Johannesburg, Lerato has been creating emotive portraiture for over 8 years. Her work explores themes of identity, heritage, and human connection through a contemporary lens. Each piece is a conversation between the subject and the viewer.

Her signature cracked glass technique was developed during a residency in Cape Town, where she experimented with layering acrylics to create texture that mimics the fragility and resilience of human emotion.
